Salon Policies

How we run
the salon.

Effective August 16, 2026  ·  Version 2026-08-16
Red Persimmon Nails & Spa
1500 South Willow Street, Manchester, NH 03103  ·  (603) 621-7469

We want every visit to feel unhurried and easy. These are the few things that help us keep it that way — please have a read before your appointment.

Booking and walk-ins

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead guarantees your time and your preferred technician, which we'd recommend for weekends and evenings.

You can book online up to 28 days ahead, and at least 2 hours before your appointment. For anything sooner, just call us.

Coming as a group

For three or more people, please call us at (603) 621-7469 rather than booking online.

A group needs several technicians free at the same time. We arrange that by hand so you're all seated together and nobody is left waiting — the booking form can't do that for you. Give us a little notice and we'll set it up properly.

For yourself or two of you, book online as normal.

Arriving

Please come a few minutes early so we can get you settled and talk through what you'd like.

If you're running late, call us — we'll do whatever we can. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or find you another time, because the person booked after you is waiting. We'll always talk it through with you first.

Prices

About half our services are priced "from" a starting figure, because the final cost depends on what you choose — nail length and shape, how detailed a design is, whether you need a soak-off or repair first, and the condition of your natural nails.

The price you see when booking is an estimate, not a fixed quote. Here's our promise:

  • Your technician tells you the cost before starting
  • If you change your mind partway through, they tell you what it adds before doing it
  • Nothing appears on your bill that you weren't told about first

Change your mind as often as you like. It's never a problem and it never costs you anything.

Paying

We take payment in the salon at the end of your appointment — card or cash.

No deposit is needed to book, and nothing is charged when you make a booking online.

Changing or cancelling

Use the link in your confirmation email, or call us.

We don't charge cancellation or no-show fees. We only ask that you tell us as early as you can. An empty slot is an hour a technician doesn't earn, so a quick call genuinely makes a difference to us.

If you need to change the services rather than the time, please call — we'll need to set aside the right amount of time.

One thing about rescheduling online: it releases your original slot to book the new one. If your appointment is soon, or you're attached to a particular time, call us instead and we'll move it safely for you.

Allergies, sensitivities, and health

Please tell us about anything that might affect your service — allergies, sensitive skin, an infection or injury, or if you're pregnant or diabetic. Put it in the booking notes or simply tell your technician when you arrive. Either is fine.

Occasionally we may need to adjust or decline a service if we don't think it's safe for you — an active nail or skin infection, for example. We'll always explain why. It's about looking after you.

Our services are cosmetic, not medical. If something about your nails or skin worries you, please see a doctor.

Children

We offer manicures ($20) and pedicures ($35) for children aged 10 and under — they're very welcome.

Please book on their behalf and stay with them in the salon during the appointment.

Gift certificates

Available in any amount, and they never expire. Use them for any service. They can't be exchanged for cash, and we're not able to replace a lost certificate.

Photos

We love showing off our work, and we may ask to photograph your nails for our website or social media.

We'll always ask you first, and "no" is a perfectly good answer — it makes no difference to your service. If we've shared a photo and you'd like it taken down, just email us.

If you're not happy

Please tell us. Come back within 7 days and we'll put it right, normally by redoing the work at no charge.

We'd far rather hear from you directly than read about it later. We take it seriously when someone leaves unhappy.

Looking after each other

Our technicians are skilled professionals and we ask that they're treated with respect, as they'll treat you. We reserve the right to decline service to anyone who is abusive to our staff or other guests.

Questions

Call (603) 621-7469 or email redpersimmon.bookings@gmail.com. You can also ask Aivy, our website assistant — though for anything important, we'd rather you spoke to a person.
